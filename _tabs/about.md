## Hey, I'm Josh 👋

I'm a tech enthusiast who spends most of his time **building**, **breaking**, and **automating** things. I work across **cloud computing**, **networking**, **security**, and **infrastructure** — and when I'm not doing that professionally, I'm doing it at home in my **homelab** for fun.

This blog is where I document my projects, share what I've learned, and hopefully help someone else avoid the same mistakes I've made along the way.

---

## What I Work With

- 🖥️ **Homelab** — Proxmox, Docker, VMs, and way too many services running on hardware that probably should have been retired years ago
- ⚙️ **Automation** — Ansible, Semaphore, cron jobs, and anything that means I don't have to do the same thing twice
- ☁️ **Cloud** — AWS, S3, Bedrock, and bridging the gap between homelab and cloud
- 🔒 **Security** — Fail2Ban, Tailscale, VPNs, and keeping things locked down
- 🌡️ **IoT** — ESP32 projects, Raspberry Pi sensors, and making hardware talk to software
- 🐧 **Linux** — Ubuntu, Debian, and the occasional battle with LVM

---

## Why This Blog?

I started writing because I kept solving the same problems and forgetting how I fixed them. Turns out, if you write it down, future-you is very grateful. If it helps someone else too, even better.
