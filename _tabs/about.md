I'm a cloud infrastructure and systems engineer with over eight years of
experience specialising in the design, implementation, and operation of
large-scale distributed systems.

I currently work at **AWS** as a Systems Engineer on Amazon S3, where I build
tooling to automate fleet remediation, regional resource deployment, and
operational processes across globally distributed infrastructure.

I'm a passionate advocate for observability, automation, and operational
excellence. I maintain an extensive home lab across four geographically
distributed sites with separate WAN links — covering virtualisation,
containerisation, networking, security, and monitoring — and write about it
here.
